Compile target.mind

Turn your poster image into the MindAR tracking file, entirely in this browser — nothing is uploaded to any server. The image is processed locally with the official MindAR compiler.

Drop poster image here or click to choose
  1. Pick the same image you will physically print as the poster (here: ../assets/poster/poster.jpg).
  2. Click Compile; when it finishes, target.mind downloads automatically.
  3. Move that file to ar/assets/poster/target.mind (replace the placeholder).
  4. Reload the poster app and choose Launch AR. Done.

Alternative: the hosted tool at hiukim.github.io/mind-ar-js-doc/tools/compile.
