18 — Other Work: Routes That Add Transport

Everything above is one family: a local rule that rearranges the heat the replay already has, without inventing any motion. The sections that follow belong to a different line of work. Each of them adds transport the recording does not contain — a circulation drawn at the lid, a mixed-layer depth over which heat is spread, a stored flux applied as a divergence, or a pair exchange that moves content between cells.

They are kept because they were run and measured, and several of them reach larger capture numbers than the family does. But they answer a different question, and mixing them into the comparison above would make the family look worse or better for reasons that have nothing to do with the family. They are collected here so the two lines stay apart.

Two things are worth carrying over. All of these were scored before the reference's own lid term was measured (§16), so their capture numbers sit on the same compressed scale as the earlier sections, and none of them has been repeated on a matched sink. And the field measurements still stand on their own, because those compare pictures rather than ratios.
