References

  1. T. Lichtenegger and S. Pirker, Recurrence CFD — a novel approach to simulate multiphase flows with strongly separated time scales, Chemical Engineering Science 153, 394–410 (2016).
  2. S. Pirker and T. Lichtenegger, Efficient time-extrapolation of single- and multiphase simulations by transport-based recurrence CFD (rCFD), Chemical Engineering Science 188, 65–83 (2018). §2.4 describes the one-step cell-to-cell exchange used for face-swap diffusion, and the separate "global balance safety belt" that uses the same exchange to keep the global mass conserved. §3.2 reports a fluidised-bed heat-transfer case where transport-based rCFD smears out the sharp, localised heat-transfer layer.
  3. H. Lumetzberger, S. Pirker and T. Lichtenegger, Propagator-moments approximation for recurrence CFD: application to species transport in turbulent flows, Chemical Engineering Science 311, 121624 (2025). Modern propagator-theoretic derivation of the face-swap operator.
  4. R. J. Hyndman and A. B. Koehler, Another look at measures of forecast accuracy, International Journal of Forecasting 22(4), 679–688 (2006). Standard reference for the bias, MAE, RMSE error metrics used here.
  5. S. Abbasi, S. Pirker and T. Lichtenegger, Application of recurrence CFD (rCFD) to species transport in turbulent vortex shedding, Computers and Fluids 196, 104348 (2020). Source of the $D_\text{rec} = D + D_\text{sgs}$ decomposition motivating the local-diffusion path.
  6. ANSYS Inc., ANSYS Fluent Theory Guide, Release 2022 R2, Canonsburg, PA (2022).
  7. Particulate Flow Modelling, JKU Linz, rCFDreloaded source code, internal repository (2026). Implementation reference for the face-swap operator (src/rCFD_swap.h).
  8. J. P. Boris and D. L. Book, Flux-Corrected Transport. I. SHASTA, a fluid transport algorithm that works, Journal of Computational Physics 11(1), 38–69 (1973). Origin of the no-new-extremum rule used by the sharpening concept in §9.4.
  9. S. T. Zalesak, Fully multidimensional Flux-Corrected Transport algorithms for fluids, Journal of Computational Physics 31(3), 335–362 (1979). Multidimensional form of the flux limiter.
  10. X. Zhang, M. Thumfart, R. Rössler and S. Pirker, Aligning real-time recurrence computational fluid dynamics simulations with plant data for the Ruhrstahl–Heraeus degassing process of steel, Steel Research International 2025, 1–16, doi:10.1002/srin.202500395. Shortening the interval between shifts as an alignment correction when a replay reproduces the character of a case but not its magnitude.
